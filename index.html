⚡ WORK

W = F × d
  • W = Work (Joules, J)  |  F = Force (Newtons, N)  |  d = distance (meters, m)
  • Work is done only when a force causes motion in the direction of the force
  • No movement = no work (scientifically!)

⚙️ MECHANICAL ADVANTAGE

MA = Output Force ÷ Input Force
MA = Input Distance ÷ Output Distance
  • Input force = force YOU apply (effort)
  • Output force = force the machine applies on the load
  • MA > 1 → machine multiplies your force (trade-off: more distance)

🪝 LEVERS

  • Class 1: Fulcrum in the middle → seesaw, crowbar, scissors
  • Class 2: Load in the middle → wheelbarrow, nutcracker, bottle opener
  • Class 3: Effort in the middle → tweezers, fishing rod, baseball bat, broom

🔄 PULLEYS

  • Fixed: Changes direction of force only · MA = 1 (flagpole)
  • Movable: Attached to load · MA = 2 (half the force needed)
  • Block & Tackle: Combined pulleys · MA = number of rope sections supporting load

🛠️ SIMPLE MACHINES & TRADE-OFFS

  • All simple machines: less force needed → more distance traveled (same total work)
  • Machines don't reduce the amount of work — they make it easier
  • Inclined plane / wedge / screw = variations of the inclined plane
